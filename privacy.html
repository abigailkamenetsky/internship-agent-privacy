Privacy Policy

internship app yayyy · Effective October 19, 2026

Who uses this app

internship app yayyy is a personal tool built by Abigail Kamenetsky for her own internship search. Its only user is its owner. It is not offered to the public.

What Google data it accesses, and why

  • Gmail, read only: reads emails under the owner's "Job Leads" label (job alert emails) to find internship postings.
  • Gmail, send: sends a daily summary email to the owner's own address. It does not email anyone else.
  • Google Sheets: writes the owner's application tracker spreadsheet.
  • Google Drive (app-created files only): stores files the app itself creates, such as the tracker. It cannot see other Drive files.

How the data is stored

Job postings extracted from emails and the application history are stored in the owner's private GitHub repository and on her own computer. Email contents are processed only to extract job postings and are not kept beyond that.

Sharing

Data is not sold, shared, or transferred to anyone. It is not used for advertising, and it is not used to train any machine learning model. Job posting text may be processed by the owner's own Claude account to evaluate fit and prepare her applications.

Limited Use

This app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deletion

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored data is deleted by deleting the private repository.
